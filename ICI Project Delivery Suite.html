Projects don't usually fail because contractors lack spreadsheets. They fail when critical information is scattered, outdated, or difficult to see.

Budgets in one file, change orders in an email thread, purchase orders on someone's desktop. Every project ends up running a little differently, and the answers you need come too late.

One system. Eight modules.

Every module covers one part of the project lifecycle, so the same structure works from startup to closeout.

Planning

Charter, scope and schedule set up in one place.

Cost

Budget, actual costs, change orders and invoices.

Procurement

Bids, contracts, purchase orders and materials.

Progress

Daily and weekly reports, lookaheads, milestones.

Risk & quality

Risks, issues, inspections and non-conformance.

Safety

Safety reports, incidents, permits and audits.

Documentation

Minutes, site instructions, correspondence, transmittals.

Closeout

Punch list, as-builts, handover, lessons learned.
